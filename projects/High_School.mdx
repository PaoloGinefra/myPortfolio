# High School
I attended the "Liceo Scientifico Arcangelo Scacchi" in Bari, Italy, where I graduated with `100 Cum Laude/100`.
My highschool shaped my foundations in an horizontal fashion but still leaving enough space for vertical growth in the subjects I was most passionate about.

It also offered a wide range of extracurricular activities that helped me develop my interests further.

## My track
I chose to enroll in a special track called "Potenziamento laboratoriale" (Laboratory Enhancement), which consisted in two additional hours per week for the first two years of the program.
This extra time was dedicated to lab hours of Physics and Chemistry.

I would say that the experiments were quite simple, but they were designed to help us understand the scientific method and the importance of experimentation.
We were taught a structured process to handle experiments: formulate an hypothesis from theoretical foundations, design an experiment and clearly state an expected result, perform the experiment in the lab and collect the data as best you can, analyze the data, make a thorough report describing the whole process and your conclusions.

At first I was frustrated by all these "chores" that often got in the way of just putting the lab coat on and playing with the glassware, but with every late night spent piecing together ideas into a coherent report, wondering about the principles that motivated the experiments in the first place and finding reasons for their consistent discrepancy from expectations,  I began to understand the true value of the process.

I joined this program on its first iteration and much was still "experimental" (pun intended), but I was really honored when at the end of my two years the professors asked me for the template I gradually perfected for lab report, and I am always pleased when my younger colleagues tell me that it is still in use.
